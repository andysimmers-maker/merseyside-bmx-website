RACING REGIONALS

HOW IT WORKS

WHO CAN RACE

Riders racing regionals need British Cycling membership with a race licence. If you've raced our club series and you're comfortable on the gate, you're ready to give it a go.

Not sure if your rider is ready? Ask a coach at any session.

ENTERING

Entries are made online through the British Cycling website. Each round above shows its entry deadline, and an ENTER ON BRITISH CYCLING button once entries open. Late entries aren't accepted, so don't leave it to the last minute.

THE RACE TEAM

Merseyside riders race regionals as a club team. Wear club kit, come and find the team at the venue, and cheer on your teammates.

Questions about the regional or national race teams go to our Race Team Manager at merseysidebmx@gmail.com.

ON THE DAY

Bring a full-face helmet, long sleeves, long trousers and gloves, plus food, water and layers for a long day outside. Practice times and team details for each round come from the Race Team Manager before race day.

Need kit? See our Bikes & Kit Guide and the Club Shop.

NEW TO RACING? Start with our club race series and the New Rider Guide. Regionals are the natural next step.
