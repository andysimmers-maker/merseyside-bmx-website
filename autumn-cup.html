CATEGORIES

EXPERT & NOVICE

The Autumn Cup runs two categories, so riders race others at a similar stage of their racing development.

CATEGORY
NOVICE

For riders earlier in their racing journey. A great environment to build race craft and confidence at club pace.

CATEGORY
EXPERT

For more experienced racers ready to push their pace against other club riders at a higher level.

NOT SURE WHICH CATEGORY? Ask a coach if you're unsure which category applies to you, or if you're new to racing. Everyone starts somewhere.
TROPHIES

RACE 3 OF 4, EARN A TROPHY

You don't need to race every round. Race in at least three of the four rounds and you'll earn a trophy in your category at the end of the series.

MISS A ROUND? NO PROBLEM You only need to race 3 of the 4 rounds to qualify, so missing one due to illness, holidays or a clash won't stop you earning a trophy.
TROPHIES Every rider who races at least 3 of the 4 rounds earns a trophy in their category. It's not a knockout, everyone who qualifies gets one.
