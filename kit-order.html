HOW IT WORKS

  1. FILL IN THE FORM

    Complete the order form and submit it. One form per rider.

  2. CHECK YOUR EMAIL

    You'll get an automatic confirmation email, so make sure your email address is correct.

  3. GET YOUR PAYMENT LINK

    We'll send you a SumUp payment link by email or text for the full amount. These are sent manually, so it may take a little while.

    Pay by 11:59pm on Sunday 8th November or your order may be cancelled.
