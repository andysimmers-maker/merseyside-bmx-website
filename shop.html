Process

HOW IT WORKS

1
ADD TO BASKET
Select your products, choose sizes and enter your rider's details: the name for the jersey, or the name, race number and age for race plates.
2
PAY SECURELY
Checkout via SumUp. Payment is taken immediately and your order is logged with the club.
3
BULK ORDER
Your order is added to the club's quarterly bulk order with our supplier. All personalisation is handled by us.
4
COLLECTION
We'll let you know when your items arrive and arrange collection at the track.
Please note: Orders are placed with our supplier every quarter. Once your payment is made, your spot in the next order is confirmed. Allow 3–5 weeks from the date the club places the order for delivery to the club.

Race Plate Stickers

Sets the number panel colours. Male / Open: yellow background, black numbers. Female: blue background, white numbers. Cruiser (male and female): red background, white numbers.
Small fits mini and junior size number plates. Large fits standard (pro) size plates. Not sure which your rider has? Ask a coach at the track before ordering.
Printed next to the Union Jack exactly as you type it. Full name, first name or surname are all fine.
Either the last 3 digits of your British Cycling membership number (e.g. 2001234 is 234), or your national ranking number from last season (e.g. 10).
Keep any zeros at the start: if your BC number ends in 001, enter 001, not 1. The plate is printed exactly as entered.
Your rider's age on 31st December of the racing year. For next season's plates, use the age they will be on 31/12/.
