NEW RIDER GUIDE

Everything you need to know for race day at Merseyside BMX Club: paying and registering, how the racing format works, series scoring and a full walkthrough of Sqorz.

SECTION 1

PAYMENT & REGISTRATION

Registration and practice times can vary from round to round, so always check the event page on Sqorz for the exact times before you travel.

NOVICE RIDERS
PAY & REGISTER AT THE CABIN

Pay at the registration desk in the green cabin. For novice riders, this also registers you for the round.

EXPERT RIDERS
PAY AT THE CABIN, REGISTER VIA TRANSPONDER

Pay at the green cabin as normal. You're registered automatically via your transponder during practice.

CHECK THE EVENT PAGE Registration and practice times are shown on the event page on Sqorz (see the Sqorz guide below for where to find it). Always check before you travel.
SECTION 2

HOW RACING WORKS

Novice and expert classes are split by age. Groups may be merged on the day depending on rider numbers, but overall series ranking is always determined by class.

RIDERS IN CLASS
FORMAT
8 or fewer
Grand Prix of 4 races (Motos). Each rider earns points based on their position in each Moto. Lowest points total after 4 races wins.
9 to 16
3 Motos. After 3 Motos, the top 8 riders go into the A Final and the rest go into the B Final. Final positions determine overall standings.
LARGER FIELDS This won't affect our club series, but larger fields (17 or more riders) run extra knockout rounds first, to cut the field down to 16 riders for the A and B finals.
SECTION 3

OVERALL SERIES

Riders earn points based on their finishing position at each round. Your best 3 rounds count towards your final points total, which determines your series finishing position.

SECTION 4

RACE & SQORZ GUIDE

We use Sqorz to run registration, race draws and results. Here's how to find your way around it on race day.

The club's Sqorz page, where you can browse events, previous results and series leaderboards.

From the club page you can open the current event, for example Autumn Cup Round 1, view previous events, and see series leaderboards.

Each class shows the number of riders entered, which decides Grand Prix vs Moto format (see above).
Key timing and pricing info is on the event page.
BEFORE THE EVENT Check your rider's details on Sqorz to make sure their plate number, transponder number and class are all correct.
ON THE DAY

Once Sqorz is updated, check your rider's page. You'll see a green tick next to Registered once they're signed in, and another once their transponder has been picked up by the system.

A rider's page, showing registration and transponder ticks, Moto draws, race times and positions.
FINDING YOUR RACE AND GATE

Once practice is complete, the races (Motos) are drawn. On your rider's page you'll see each Moto with two numbers separated by a colon.

6:6
The first number is the race number, the second is the gate.
FINDING YOUR PEN

There are 10 pens. Riders go to the pen matching the last digit of their race number.

Race 6
last digit
Pen 6
Race 14
last digit
Pen 4

Our pen marshals will call each race forward and tell riders when to go to the start hill. Riders line up in the gate shown as the second number on Sqorz.

RESULTS

Moto results appear on Sqorz shortly after each race. Riders with transponders will see their gate time and lap time, with final positions available very quickly.

NOVICE RESULTS Novice race positions are only finalised once results have been taken from the finish line to the Sqorz operator. This may not happen until after the round is complete, so don't worry if novice positions aren't showing straight away.
Class results, showing ranking, points and each Moto's race:gate, time and position.

After each Moto round there's a short break before riders are recalled to the pens for the next race.

SECTION 5

ABOVE ALL, ENJOY IT

Race days are about having fun, gaining experience and enjoying racing as a family, not just chasing results. Every gate drop makes you a better rider, whatever the result.

HELP US PACK DOWN Once the last final has run, staying behind for a few minutes to help tidy up and pack away equipment makes a huge difference. The quicker we all pitch in, the quicker everyone gets home.

NEED A HAND?

Don't worry if it all feels like a lot to take in. There are plenty of experienced volunteers and riders around on the day who are always happy to help. Just ask!
